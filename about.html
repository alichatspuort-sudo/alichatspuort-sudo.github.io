About this ledger

The Tool Ledger is a running, day-by-day record of AI tools worth knowing about. Each entry covers one tool: what it actually does, who it's useful for, and a small set of prompts you can copy and use right away — no fluff, no affiliate spin.

New AI tools launch every week and most "top 10 AI tools" lists are outdated within a month. This ledger takes a slower, more honest approach: one tool at a time, tested and explained in plain language, added to the log permanently so it stays searchable and useful over time.

Who writes this

The Tool Ledger is written and maintained by an independent automation practitioner who works with AI tools daily and wanted a simple place to track the useful ones.

How entries are chosen

Tools are picked based on genuine day-to-day usefulness rather than popularity or sponsorship. If a tool is included here, it's because it earned a place in the log, not because it paid for one.
